# 1.0 Ways of Working

## Chapter Goals

 1. Be able to describe DevOps in your own words.
 2. Know how Liatrio works: The Liatrio Way, its First Principles, the Tao of Liatrio, and our Engineering Defaults.
 3. Become introduced to agile development practices and how teams plan and track work.
 4. Experience both solo development and pair programming.
 5. Explore some other resources for learning about DevOps.
