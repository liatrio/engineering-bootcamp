# 1.0 Ways of Working

## Chapter Goals

 1. Become introduced to agile development practices and how teams plan and track work.
 2. Experience both solo development and pair programming.
 3. Be able to describe DevOps in your own words.
 4. Know how Liatrio works: The Liatrio Way, its First Principles, the Tao of Liatrio, and our Engineering Defaults.
 5. Explore some other resources for learning about DevOps.
