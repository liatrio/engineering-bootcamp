# Liatrio and DevOps

This might be your first apprenticeship; if so, congratulations! You might get this question at some point.

> "What do you all _do_?"

Well...

Liatrio is an AI enablement firm for complex businesses. For more than a decade we've worked inside large enterprises, helping engineering and business teams work better together. Our forward deployed engineers embed inside client teams to improve development practices, deliver value from conception to deployment, and react more quickly to market changes, through strategy, build, and enablement work. Another way to look at it is by asking the following question.

> How long does it take for your company to deploy software with one line of code difference?

Sometimes the answer is staggering, and we try to change that.

Together, our Vision, Mission, First Principles, and the Tao of Liatrio make up **The Liatrio Way**: the shared foundation for who we are and how we work.

## Vision and Mission

**Our Vision:** Transforming the way enterprises create and deliver digital value to their customers.

**Our Mission:** We believe enterprises are capable of far more than they're currently delivering. Closing that gap has been our work for a decade, across people, process, culture, and technology. AI raises the ceiling on what's possible, and we're focused on helping enterprises reach it. That means hiring people who refuse to settle, building capabilities that go deeper every year, and staying honest with clients about what real transformation actually requires.

## How We Work

Our customers are large, established organizations in industries such as retail, healthcare, banking, entertainment, and air travel. They want to grow while fending off competition, and they're revamping their IT and software delivery so they can respond to customer needs faster.

Liatrio drives change at two levels at once:

- **With leadership**, mapping the current state, finding where delivery is breaking down, and building a roadmap around the client's outcomes.
- **With delivery teams**, as forward deployed engineers who pair with the team, demonstrate, and co-build on real work in the client's actual environment.

We're forward deployed engineers, not parachuted-in consultants or staff augmentation. We measure success by our clients' outcomes, and the goal is for clients to own and sustain their improved delivery after we leave.

AI is woven into how we work, not bolted on. We help clients adopt AI across software delivery, and we use AI tools in our own day-to-day work. You can learn more about this in the [AI Engineering](addendum/ai-engineering/ai-engineering-overview.md) section of the addendum.

## The Rise of DevOps

DevOps has revolutionized software development within the last couple decades. At the turn of the century, it was common that development teams would work on independent pieces of a project, which would ultimately have to be merged and compiled by a tech lead at the end of a project. This led to an enormous problem. If code didn't merge together and function as expected, a team might not know until very late in the development process, or even until after deploying. Since that time, developers have created processes and tools to help build, test, and manage code throughout the life of a project. Those tools and processes help form the foundation of what we know today as DevOps.

## The Goals

DevOps practices aim to take the pain out of software delivery and deployment.

1) Developers learn about code issues as soon as possible. Fast feedback loops allow the developer to fix code issues without switching to another task or forgetting how the code functions. Addressing issues as quickly as possible also maintains the code's integrity and ensures that it's always in a working state. Feedback isn't just about a program's functionality though. Good feedback loops should also let developers hear from users and help guarantee that features are actually useful to customers.

2) Automation of source code management (SCM) and build servers. SCM tools keeps everyone on the same page about the state of a code base, while build servers validate the functionality and quality of code.

3) Every build could potentially go to production. While not every change necessarily needs to be released immediately, it's valuable to have enough automation and validation that any changes _could_ go to production if desired.

4) A culture similar to that of Startups. An agile mindset. Close-knit teams. Open and transparent communication. Individual responsibility and accountability.

## First Principles

Liatrio's five First Principles, refined through years of hands-on work, guide how we make decisions, solve problems, and work with clients. They're practical tools that every Liatrian is expected to apply day to day, and they reinforce one another. You'll practice each of them throughout this bootcamp.

### Small Batch and Fast Feedback

> Breaking down work into the smallest manageable size that produces value, accelerating workflows by focusing on user feedback and rapid iteration.

Small changes are less risky, more predictable, and easier to change course on, and every batch teaches you something. Rather than one big change, make many small ones and get each in front of users. See [Story splitting](1-ways-of-working/1.1.4-stories.md#story-splitting) and [Start small and learn](1-ways-of-working/1.1.5-scoping-and-refinement.md#start-small-and-learn).

### Culture of Experimentation and Empowerment

> An environment where every team member is encouraged to test new ideas quickly and is trusted to make decisions that drive innovation and growth.

Great ideas can come from anywhere. Take the initiative to test new ideas without waiting for permission, share what you learn, and treat failed experiments as learning. See [spikes](1-ways-of-working/1.1.6-sizing.md#what-is-a-spike).

### Direct and Transparent Communication

> A commitment to clear, honest communication that defaults to openness, bringing visibility to how work gets done across all levels of our organization and with our clients.

To be unclear is to be unkind. Keep the message consistent instead of filtering it through layers of "translation," and work in the open so others can learn and stay informed without being interrupted. Transparency doesn't mean everyone must join every conversation; say whether you're sharing for visibility or asking for input. See [Work Tracking](1-ways-of-working/1.2-work-tracking.md) and [Pair Programming](1-ways-of-working/1.3-pairprogramming.md).

### Focus on Flow

> Minimizing the time and waste from start to finish, from when someone can work on a problem until they're able to get the smallest deliverable value to a customer.

The only way to show value is to ship. That means making the end-to-end path to production seamless, automated, and sustainable: finding bottlenecks, reducing handoffs, and automating repeatable work. Value stream mapping is a common way to see where the waste is. See [Kanban](1-ways-of-working/1.1.2-kanban.md) and [Test Automation](3-application-development/3.2.7-test-automation.md).

### Default to Action

> Getting things done now, not later.

Also called _Emphasis on Action_: moving swiftly from ideas to implementation.

Avoid analysis paralysis. When an idea comes up, start sketching a prototype or writing code instead of scheduling another meeting, then use small batches and feedback to refine it. Acting quickly builds momentum, speeds up learning, and lets you adapt based on real results.

## The Tao

The Tao of Liatrio is the set of foundational values that guide our work, vision, strategy, and delivery for ourselves and our clients. We carry these philosophies in mind at all times to guide our decision making, inspire us to always bring our best selves, and to understand why we work the way we do.

### Become an Ally for Our Customers

- We challenge our customers through empathy and positive leadership. We act as change agents to help them accomplish their goals.
- Many principles and technologies we introduce are new to our customers. We don't work with our clients to "be right", we help them understand why something is right for them.
- Our customers know their business better than we do. We listen and learn in order to create mutual outcomes WITH clients, not just for them.
- We understand that we aren't the hero, we're the advisor/mentor. We're not Luke Skywalker, we're Yoda.

### Continuous Improvement

- Technology is never in a final state; it's always on a road of improvement. Transformation isn't a destination, it's a never-ending journey.
- Behavioral and cultural practices are also not permanently set. We help our customers continuously improve not only on an organizational level, but on a holistic and personal level too.
- Prioritizing improvement of a system's overall flow yields the greatest benefit in delivery improvement. Once faster flow is achieved, it's easier to solve individual problems.

### Culture of Feedback

- We advocate for fast feedback, through Proof of Concept (PoC), a Minimum Viable Product (MVP), or any other similar approach. Fast feedback allows us to gauge progress and make small, iterative moves aligned to what the feedback is signaling.
- We work to prove or disprove an idea as early as possible in order to best adjust our approach to a problem.
- While mistakes (technical and non-technical) do happen, we look at them as learning opportunities.
- Celebrating wins is equally important to teams and delivery work too. We make sure to pause and celebrate when the team wins.

### Strong Opinions, Loosely Held

- Strong Opinions means that we are confident in our positions and stand behind them -- we are passionate, motivated, and informed in our professional opinions.‍
- Loosely Held means that we are always willing to challenge our opinions and can listen to and collaborate respectfully with other points of view.

### Community Builders

- Community is critical for the success of software development. We strive to create, enhance, and build community wherever possible.
- We use collaborative tools such as Slack, GitHub, etc. in order to encourage the behavior of excessive transparency and oversharing -- thus allowing everyone to have situational awareness.
- Open source encourages community building and adds immediate value for our clients and ourselves. We embrace open source in both tools and mindset.
